## Who it's for

Homes getting ready for Christmas, fiestas or visitors; homes not deep-cleaned in 6 months or more; new regular-plan clients who want a clean starting point; households with allergies.

## What's included

Everything in a regular clean, plus:

- **Kitchen:** stove, range hood exterior and backsplash degreased; inside empty cabinets and drawers; outside of all appliances; sink and faucet descaled.
- **Bathrooms:** tile grout and corners scrubbed; soap scum and limescale removed from tiles, glass and fixtures; exhaust fan covers cleaned.
- **All rooms:** ceiling corners, fan blades, light fixtures, door frames and baseboards; window frames, sills and tracks; behind and under movable furniture.
- **Floors:** scrubbed and mopped, edges and corners included.

**Not included (add-ons):** inside fridge ₱390, inside oven ₱490, inside windows ₱290 per room. We don't move heavy wardrobes or work above 2 metres.

## How we do it

Top to bottom and back to front: high dusting first, then surfaces, then floors, so nothing falls on a cleaned area. Grout brushes, descaler for hard-water marks and degreaser for kitchens.
