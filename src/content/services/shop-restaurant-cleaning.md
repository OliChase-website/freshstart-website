## Who it's for

Salons and barbershops, nail and lash studios, milk tea and coffee shops, food stalls and small restaurants, pharmacies, retail stores, laundry shops and gyms.

## What's included each visit (2 hours)

Floors scrubbed and mopped (hair, grease and sticky spills treated), counters and display cases, glass doors and windows inside, customer CR deep-cleaned, bins emptied, chairs and tables wiped, and for food businesses the prep-area floor and walls to shoulder height.

**Not included:** cooking equipment internals, hood and duct cleaning, grease trap pumping, pest control.
