## Who it's for

Busy households, working couples, families with kids, pets or elderly parents, and anyone who no longer has live-in help and wants the house guest-ready every weekend.

## What's included on every visit

- **Kitchen:** up to one sink of dishes, counters, stove top, outside of appliances and cabinet doors, sink, bin.
- **Bathrooms:** toilet, sink, shower area and tiles scrubbed; mirrors and fixtures wiped.
- **Bedrooms and living areas:** dusting of reachable surfaces and furniture, switches and handles wiped, beds made, cushions tidied.
- **Floors:** vacuumed and mopped in every room; entrance and garage floor swept.
- **Finish:** bins emptied and a photo report.

**Not included (add-ons):** inside fridge ₱390, inside oven ₱490, inside windows ₱290 per room, laundry folding ₱290 per load. A home with heavy build-up may need a deep clean first.

## How we do it

Colour-coded microfiber cloths (one colour for bathrooms, another for kitchens), labelled and correctly diluted cleaning products, and a checklist the team ticks on their phone.

## What to prepare

Clear counters of items you don't want moved, secure valuables and documents, and tell us about pets and delicate surfaces.
