## What's included

- Fine dust vacuumed from ceilings, walls, ledges, cabinets and floors before any wiping.
- Light paint splatter, grout haze, cement drops and sticker residue removed from tiles, glass, frames and fixtures.
- Inside and outside of new cabinets, drawers and shelves.
- Windows, frames, sills and tracks reachable from inside.
- Bathrooms and kitchen to deep-clean standard; two-pass floor cleaning; small debris bagged.

**Not included:** construction waste and large debris, floor polishing, work above 2 metres, exterior upper-floor windows.

## Price

**₱75 per sqm, minimum ₱5,990.** Quoted from 5 to 10 photos on Messenger, or a free site visit in our service areas.

## What to prepare

Make sure the builders have taken out their tools and large debris, and tell us about delicate new finishes such as natural stone or wood.
