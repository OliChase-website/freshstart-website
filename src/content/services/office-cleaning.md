## What's included every visit

- Bins emptied and relined.
- Desks (clear areas only; we never move papers), shelves, reception and monitors wiped.
- Door handles, switches and shared phones disinfected.
- Pantry: sink, counters, microwave outside, used cups washed.
- CRs fully cleaned; carpets vacuumed; hard floors swept and mopped; glass doors spot-cleaned.

**Fresh (3× a week) adds** a monthly detail task: glass partitions, high dusting, pantry appliances or chair vacuuming. **Spotless (5× a week) adds** a quarterly deep clean and a named coordinator.
