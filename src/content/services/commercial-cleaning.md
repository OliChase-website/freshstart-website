## What is commercial cleaning?

Scheduled, professional cleaning of a business space: offices, clinics, salons, food stalls, tutorial centres and showrooms. It runs on a membership plan: 2 to 5 visits a week at set times, a written checklist for your space, and one monthly invoice.

## Janitorial service for small businesses

Our scheduled plans cover the daily work a janitor does (bins, CRs, floors, pantry, glass) without you hiring, supervising or covering for a utility worker. If your cleaner is sick, we send a trained replacement.
