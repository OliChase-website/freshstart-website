## What is a house cleaning service?

A house cleaning service sends trained cleaners to your home to do the cleaning for you: the kitchen, bathrooms, floors, dusting, beds and the small jobs that pile up during a busy week. FreshStart brings every tool and cleaning product, so you only need to open the door.

We clean single houses in Greater Lagro and Fairview, townhouses in Pasong Putik and North Fairview, apartments in Kaligayahan, and condos and rentals across Novaliches. Book us once, every two weeks or every week, and the same team comes each time.

1. **Book** on Messenger, Viber or by phone with your home size, the service and your date.

5. **You get the photo report** and pay the balance. Missed something? Tell us within 48 hours and we come back free.
