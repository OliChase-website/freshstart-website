## Who it's for

Tenants who want their deposit back, new owners moving in, landlords between tenants and agents preparing a unit for photos.

## What's included

- Everything in a deep clean, done in an empty home.
- Inside every cabinet, drawer, closet and shelf.
- Inside the fridge and oven, if left in the unit, at no extra charge.
- Wall spot-cleaning of marks and scuffs.
- Light fixtures, switch plates, doors and door frames; balcony scrub; parking slot sweep.
- **Dated photo report** of every room, plus a list of damage or repairs we noticed.
